## Prompt comum e cenas (texto enviado à ferramenta)

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: square explanatory picture button for a communication app used by preschool children who cannot read. Input image 1 is the EXACT main-character reference: preserve the same friendly golden yellow fox/cat mascot, huge triangular pink inner ears, swept golden forelock, large black-and-white eyes, tiny black nose, fluffy golden tail, light blue V-neck T-shirt and light blue shorts. Keep character identity and clothing consistent. Polished soft 3D children's book illustration matching reference, expressive pose, clean forms, high legibility at small size. One main mascot only. Square composition, pale warm cream background, generous safe margins, character and relevant action fill 85% of square, simple props with saturated clear colors, no decorative clutter, no border. No written words, NO letters, no numerals, no captions, no watermark. Pictograms must be concrete recognizable objects. Scene:
