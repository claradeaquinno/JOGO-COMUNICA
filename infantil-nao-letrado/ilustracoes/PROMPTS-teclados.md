## Prompt comum

Use case: illustration-story. Square minimal explanatory illustration for NONREADING CHILDREN in a communication app. Input image is the exact character reference: friendly buttery golden-yellow fox mascot, huge triangular pink inner ears, swept tuft, black-and-white oval eyes, tiny black nose, golden tail, powder-blue V-neck shirt and shorts. Preserve identity. Soft matte cartoon children's illustration, diffuse lighting, simple navy outlines #26485D. Muted app palette powder blue #BFDEEF, pale buttery yellow #F6E5AC, sage #BFD9C6, pale almost-white cream background #F7FAF8. No neon, saturated red, orange or purple; no shiny plastic. ONE mascot plus ONE communication keyboard interface. Mascot visibly USING the interface with a clear touch gesture. Interface very large and front facing, distinctive layout readable at 150px, few elements, ample empty space. No writing, letters, numerals, captions, watermark, scenery, furniture or decoration.
